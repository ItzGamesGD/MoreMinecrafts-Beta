## Candidate content identities

1. **Fishing habitat mastery:** refine the existing twelve species' ecology and discovery across freshwater, cool rivers/coasts, warm rivers, and oceans. Reuse Reel access, Minnow permission, Crankbait grade, and existing species fields. No new currency or fish abstraction.
2. **Brewing discovery:** add hints and compact documentation for current recipes before considering new potion effects. Reuse vanilla Brewing Stand interactions; do not add a research station.
3. **Vanilla milestone activities:** validate the five-advancement pilot, then add only milestones that close an uncovered normal Minecraft action. Keep rewards fixed, modest, one-time, and per-player.
4. **Approved mob identity pass:** finish art and live encounter tuning for the current forty before deciding whether any additional enemy adds a genuinely new combat role. Mire Shaman and Shard Golem remain later candidates, not committed content.
5. **One End encounter:** retain the unified End-boss concept for later. Lock its fight identity and reward before tying recipes, materials, or structure work to it.

No new axis or candidate roster is approved by this review. The next order is: complete automated/code audit -> live-test current loops -> select a content identity and roster -> define its reward/source and equipment relationship -> aggregate material demand -> address world/structure expansion last.
