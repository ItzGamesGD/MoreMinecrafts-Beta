## Reading rules

APPROVED means an explicit approval is recorded by the cited source, only to its stated scope. PROPOSED means unapproved candidate/recommendation or technical plan. DEFERRED means deliberately postponed or left open. REJECTED means explicitly excluded. SUPERSEDED identifies a prior rule replaced by a cited explicit decision. SOURCE MISSING means the asserted original approval/mechanic cannot be recovered from inspected sources. Document titles, dates, code, Draft status and copying do not independently establish approval. Where two apparently current approvals cannot be reconciled by an explicit amendment, keep the conflict open; recommendations below are not owner decisions.

All 24 original documents are preserved. [Coverage](DESIGN_BRANCH_COVERAGE.md) records each immutable source, original/last-edit commits, byte identity and all branch snapshots. [Conflict register](DESIGN_CONFLICT_REGISTER.md) records blockers; [phase plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PHASE_PLAN.md) proposes future PR boundaries. This matrix routes evidence rather than replacing unrelated current authority.
