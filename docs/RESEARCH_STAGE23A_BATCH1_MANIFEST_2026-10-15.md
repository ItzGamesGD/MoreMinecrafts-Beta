## Explicit exclusions from Batch 1

- `v3.research.07` Precision Smelting: approved effect retained, but excluded until the mineral-input eligibility boundary for vanilla and modded smelting and the exact output-creation hook are verified. A result-slot pickup hook would be too late and could duplicate or reroll output.
- `v3.research.08` Precision Crafting: approved effect retained, but its one-input-slot preservation, rarity classes, remainders, and shift-click transaction guard are a separate crafting transaction feature.
- `v3.research.14` Crop Cultivation: approved probabilities retained, but crop-output compatibility mapping is broader than this batch's simple seed-drop modifier.
- `v3.research.19` Fisheries Study: approved grade tables retained; unattended fish-trap research ownership/attribution needs separate verification.
- Stateful devices, GUIs, transport, Tactical Shooting, and cross-dimensional transport are outside scope.
