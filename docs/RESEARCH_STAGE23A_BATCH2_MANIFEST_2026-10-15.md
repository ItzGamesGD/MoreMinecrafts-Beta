## Shared constraints

- The paid V3 prerequisite/cost catalog is already present. Do not modify costs, prerequisites, topology, or unrelated rewards.
- All effects run server-side and gate against the player receiving or initiating the event. Player-specific state must not leak across players or disconnects.
- Preserve vanilla results and award/XP conservation apart from the approved research bonus. Do not recurse through bonus offspring, XP, fish rolls, or attacks.
- No resource files, recipes, GUIs, persistent block entities, transport, or new progression systems are planned.
- Verify mapped game methods for livestock breeding and XP pickup before wiring injections; if a method boundary cannot preserve the approved event semantics, record the blocker and replace that node only with another complete, bounded candidate.
