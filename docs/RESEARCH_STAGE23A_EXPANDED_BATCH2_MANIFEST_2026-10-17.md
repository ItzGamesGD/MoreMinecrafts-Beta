## Compatibility repairs

Two custom cave-spider variants previously removed and restored an existing poison via `addEffect`. With duration research active, restoration would apply a second adjustment. `SmallCaveSpider` and `SwampCaveSpider` now restore that existing instance with vanilla `forceAddEffect`, retaining its prior duration and hidden state. A regression test reproduced the prior double adjustment. The duration hook also privately copies the entire incoming hidden-effect chain so it cannot lose a weaker queued effect or mutate a shared AoE input.
