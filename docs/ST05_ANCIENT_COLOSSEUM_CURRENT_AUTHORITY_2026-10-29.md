# ST-05 Ancient Colosseum — current design authority (Q1–Q110)

**Status: OWNER-APPROVED DESIGN ONLY. Implementation, merge, checkpoint promotion and live acceptance are not authorized by this record.** This is the effective ST-05 specification reconciled onto Wave15 c9f7a4bf from PR #207 head dd193d78 and the owner-provided Q109 roster table. [Decision history](ST05_ANCIENT_COLOSSEUM_DECISION_HISTORY_2026-10-29.md) preserves the original wording, including revoked options; it is not an implementation source. [Structure authority index](STRUCTURE_AUTHORITY_INDEX.md) routes here. ST-05 is separate from the Ancient City arena (Phase 2 F-6).

## Structure and arena (Q26–Q32, Q54–Q59, Q84, Q94–Q95)

- Generate a rare, large, ruined Roman-style colosseum only inside a sufficiently enormous **natural Overworld cave**, deep underground. It is never a surface structure. Its architecture is mainly deepslate-family blocks with damaged masonry and stands; restrained natural cave decoration is allowed. Keep the constructed, generally flat central fighting floor brightly lit. Stands and upper seating are decorative, not encounter routes.
- Provide four **player** entrances, north/east/south/west. They are not mob spawn gates. Players may discover the structure through caves or a dedicated exploration map obtained by existing vanilla-style systems, including cartographer trade as an example. The map is not required for access.
- The combat zone is the central floor. A small glowing activation crystal floats above the exact-center Ominous Vault without obstructing interaction, movement or center item pickup. Entering the center starts round 1 automatically, signalled by blue particles; no click is required. Crystal states: soft glow ready, stronger glow active, dim/dark glow on cooldown.
- For rounds 1–6, mob spawn candidates lie inside the combat floor in a **12–24 block radial annulus around the fixed arena center**, independent of player positions. Require solid supporting floor, enough collision clearance for the selected entity, and no wall or immediate hazard intersection. Each selected valid point receives about one second of lightweight particle warning before an initial or replenishment spawn. Do not derive spawns from the four player entrances.
- Exact worldgen spacing, cave-fit thresholds, combat-zone geometry, vertical spawn bounds, map trade terms, visuals and sampling remain implementation/balance work; no unapproved numeric value is supplied here.

## Trial lifecycle and participation (Q1–Q25, Q60–Q62, Q83, Q97–Q107)

- Run **exactly seven sequential rounds**: R1 ordinary, R2 varied ordinary, R3 special mixed, R4 enhanced ordinary, R5 elite, R6 enhanced elite, R7 Immortal Gladiator. R1 begins on center approach. After ordinary round clearance, wait **10 seconds** and start the next automatically. After R3 and R5, make supplies accessible during a **35-second** interval, then start the next automatically. R7 begins after R6's 10-second interval; the boss materializes at the center with converging particles and a sword-and-shield awakening. R7 ends on boss defeat, with ordinary mob-death presentation. R1–R6 have no dedicated round/progress HUD; R7 has a conventional boss health bar that disappears at encounter end.
- Physical entry allows immediate combat and physical exit immediately ends that player's active combat presence. Trial-system participation, scaling, supply eligibility/count, rewards, elimination and rejoin eligibility take effect only from rounds starting after entry; do not retroactively modify a round's locked allocation. At every round start, lock the eligible participant count for its difficulty. Spatial presence in the combat zone, never damage contribution, determines physical presence and kill-credit eligibility.
- If all participants are absent, pause **trial progression and new spawn batches** for up to **60 seconds**. Existing trial mobs retain normal AI, movement and attacks. Return within grace resumes the encounter; expiry resets it. One participant remaining avoids this pause. Total-party defeat fails the run; a surviving teammate may continue after another dies. A dead participant can rejoin only after the current round clears and before the next starts. External/naturally spawned mobs may enter and fight but never count for budgets, wave objectives or trial cleanup.
- Trial-owned mobs killed by traps, environment or farms count toward their wave regardless of attacker **only if at least one player is physically inside the combat zone when the kill is evaluated**. No wave advances during an empty-arena grace period. On failure/reset, remove only this trial instance's spawned mobs and boss, without death loot or XP; preserve external mobs. Remove this run's generated supply chests and unclaimed contents on success/failure/reset, but retain player-taken supplies and player construction. Never reclaim awarded Trial Keys, including center item entities.
- Start a **65-minute / 78,000-tick** cooldown on success at boss death, or on conclusive failure/reset (including expiry of the absence grace period). It applies to all three outcomes. The crystal reflects cooldown. Do not substitute the obsolete 30-minute trial cooldown.

## Threat budget, roster and spawns (Q21–Q25, Q33–Q49, Q67–Q68, Q77–Q78, Q107–Q110)

- R1–R2 solo budget **20** each, R3–R4 **32** each, R5–R6 **48** each. For n participants, multiply by 1 + 0.60 × (n−1) and round to the nearest integer. The additional-player headcount increase is a target, not an exact guaranteed count; threat budget takes priority. For 1/2/3/4 participants, budgets are respectively R1–R2 **20/32/44/56**, R3–R4 **32/51/70/90**, R5–R6 **48/77/106/134**.
- Select randomized compositions from cumulative score eligibility: R1–R2 scores **1–4**, R3–R4 **1–7**, R5–R6 **1–10**. Favor lower scores early, medium scores in the middle and stronger scores late without excluding lower scores later. Normally fill **ceil(85% × rounded budget) through 100%**. Only if no normal valid composition exists, allow exceptional total up to **floor(105% × rounded budget)**. If neither band is possible, choose the highest valid total at or below the base budget even if below 85%. An empty/unusable pool is data-validation failure, never an excuse to borrow another round's roster.
- R3 has melee, ranged and special-ability roles with **exactly one focal elite** included inside its budget. When score ≥7 defines an elite, no second score-7-or-higher mob may appear in R3. R5–R6 draw randomized elite compositions; no numeric elite-density or concurrent-elite cap is approved beyond the overall 12-alive limit.
- Cap **simultaneously living trial-owned ordinary/elite mobs at 12**. Full-round allocation may exceed 12. When alive trial mobs fall to **6 or fewer**, replenish pending allocations in a batch up to the 12-alive cap. Spawned, pending, valid-kill and currently alive counts must remain distinct. A failed position check never consumes an allocation; retry retained allocations on the next normal batch-spawn cycle. No independent fixed-interval retry loop is approved.
- **Q110:** If allocations remain unspawned and *all otherwise eligible spawn positions* stay invalid for **60 consecutive seconds of active trial time**, fail the encounter, remove this trial's mobs and generated supply chests, preserve already awarded keys, and apply the 78,000-tick cooldown. Do not advance the blockage clock while spawning is legitimately held by the 12-living-mob cap or while all players are absent and the controller is paused. Reset the consecutive clock as soon as a valid spawn position exists. Never complete a round because a pending mob could not spawn or because a trial mob disappeared without an eligible kill.
- Existing vanilla and MoreMinecrafts mobs are used; the Immortal Gladiator is the only new mob designed for this encounter. Scores below are provisional until real-play tuning. Initial targeting may be seeded to the nearest player inside the arena as a spawn correction, with functional native AI targeting verified and no new attack behavior.

## Supplies, keys and Vaults (Q10–Q15, Q50–Q51, Q63–Q66 as corrected, Q81–Q82, Q85, Q96–Q99)

- After **R3 and R5** generate **one public supply chest per participating player** in randomized safe positions around the arena. Each chest has identical NBT and fixed contents: **8 cooked beef, 16 arrows, one ordinary golden apple**. Contents do not receive the 60% participant multiplier. Chests are shared/public, not assigned to individual players. No repeat claim/farming is authorized. Remove only trial-generated chests and leftovers on trial end; never remove unrelated player chests.
- There are **seven Vaults total**: **six ordinary vanilla Vaults** distributed uniformly around the central area and **one vanilla Ominous Vault at the exact center**. They retain vanilla independent, per-player key redemption and can be opened whenever a player is otherwise eligible; no wave-based lock is added. The activation crystal floats above the center Vault.
- Current wave rewards are **keys only**: one normal Trial Key per completed wave, dropped as an item entity at the exact arena center. No dedicated reward-ejection machine exists. Award each wave once; never reclaim awarded keys on failure/reset. In multiplayer, track player-specific trial reward eligibility to prevent duplicate payouts against the same eligibility (Q51). This does not multiply the one shared Trial Key per completed wave or change vanilla Vaults' independent per-player redemption. The exact persistence format is an implementation detail. The boss drops one Ominous Trial Key at its own feet plus any separately approved direct loot. Boss-foot loot is one shared set of ordinary item entities, with no per-player clones or last-hit ownership.
- Gladiator sword and shield are **boss combat equipment, not collectible player equipment**. Do not create their old proposed player loot. Other rare-material or actual reward items, quantities, sources and odds remain outside the approved concrete roster; do not manufacture new wave rewards or loot tables from historical proposals.

## Immortal Gladiator (Q6–Q9, Q22–Q23, Q61, Q69–Q75, Q79–Q80 as corrected, Q85–Q95)

- Prefer a **dedicated custom model and animations**, approximately **1.8–2.0× player scale**; a mannequin entity is fallback only if dedicated modeling/animation is impractical. If GeckoLib is adopted, expressive synchronized animations may be used; otherwise use restrained in-mod motions while retaining readable attack states. GeckoLib adoption itself is undecided and this document authorizes no dependency change.
- Solo maximum HP is **300**, plus **35% per additional round-7 participant** locked at round start: 1/2/3/4 players **300/405/510/615 HP**. At **40% of scaled maximum HP** (**120/162/204/246 HP** respectively), pause briefly, break/discard the shield, roar, allow a response window and enter faster shieldless sustained sword-combo phase. Exact timings remain tuning work.
- Ordinary sword hits deal **10 damage before armor mitigation against an unarmored player**. Do not add an invented barehand baseline or stack an unapproved sword bonus. Ordinary attacks use moderate vanilla-like knockback; heavy slash/charge may knock back more. Phase 1 includes sword combos, directional frontal shield blocks with flank/rear vulnerability, shield shove then sword follow-up on a successful block, lunge/thrust, and broad horizontal slash. Five successful shield blocks or a qualifying player axe disable shield defense and shield counter for approximately **20–25 seconds**, while ordinary sword fighting continues. The shield breaks permanently at phase 2.
- The distinct powerful charging slash has a readable sword-raise warning, straight-line charge and heavy strike. Phase 2 lengthens combos and shortens gaps without inventing ranged attacks or an automatic charge-frequency increase. Players may block ordinary sword hits with vanilla shields; blocking the powerful charging slash temporarily disables the player's shield. Its duration is a separate balance detail.
- Prefer the **nearest valid participating player**; ordinary pursuit is slightly faster than player walking. Retarget another reachable valid player when needed. If within **20 seconds** pursuit fails at least **10 times** while the boss receives one-sided attacks and cannot retaliate, allow a special obstacle-breaking charge at the nearest valid player. It has a longer visual telegraph than the ordinary charge, no new dedicated warning sound, and a **separate 30-second cooldown**. Its trigger conditions must be satisfied anew after cooldown.
- The exceptional charge may break only permitted **external natural stone/wood-type blocks**. Never break original colosseum structure blocks, floor, walls, stands, decorations, Vaults, crystal, unbreakable blocks or player construction; block-type matching alone is insufficient. Restore each eligible boss-destroyed block to its exact prior state at trial end/reset, including after server restart. Players retain normal vanilla survival block placement/breaking, including arena architecture, and their modifications persist. Player barriers/traps/farms are allowed; no blanket edit ban, forced anti-farm removal or new regular-mob block breaking is approved.
- Telegraphs use readable weapon/body wind-up, restrained particles for stronger attacks and projected danger areas before major charge/area attacks. Exact reach, movement speed, hitboxes, attack timings, particle assets and damage for special attacks remain implementation and live-balance work.
