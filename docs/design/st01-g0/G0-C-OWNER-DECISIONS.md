# G0-C — Owner decisions (grouped)

No proposal below is approved. The two conflicts must be resolved before a coherent full blueprint can be finalized.

## D1-SEAM — immutable asset versus existing hidden descent (blocking conflict)

Independent NBT decoding shows that the P4 FA cavity intersects 12 solid D1 blocks beyond the normal cover: X−8..−5, Y−24, Z33..35. Keeping D1 bytes unchanged does NOT keep these assembled blocks unchanged if the old descent is carved through them.

Options:
1. **Preferred for continuity, requires explicit exception approval:** preserve D1 file bytes and all its other blocks, but authorize a precisely enumerated assembly seam override at those 12 lower-floor cells, in addition to existing cover gameplay. Requires concealment, structural, clearance and open/closed tests. This relaxes the current assembled-block immutability requirement; it is NOT assumed permitted.
2. Preserve every D1 block in the closed assembly and redesign the concealed entrance/descent outside its reservation. This changes the approved secret entrance location and requires a new architectural approval and traversal study.

The choice affects architecture and authority, not merely export mechanics. Neither is implemented.

## SECRET-WEST — meaning of independent EAST/WEST (blocking conflict)

Existing P6 WEST was obtained by mirroring the combined design: its PRIMARY anchor is (+21,−20,33). Current D1's anchor is (−21,−20,33). Four whole rotations preserve the mismatch; no rotation fixes it. Simply translating the old WEST model to align anchors also changes component placements and bounds and is not an approved solution.

Options:
1. **Preferred to preserve D1:** retain one immutable hall and fixed treasury; separately design two concealed access routes. This redefines secret-area orientation as access orientation and needs explicit owner approval. New route, concealment, foundation and reservation checks are required; no geometry is silently invented now.
2. Preserve the requirement for two entire secret-area layouts. Author a genuinely independent WEST layout around the fixed hall, with its own corridor, treasury/crypt coordinates and clearance validation. More design work and potential reservation changes; no existing WEST export may be reused wholesale.

Do not reduce the approved 50:50 choice to an EAST-only default.
