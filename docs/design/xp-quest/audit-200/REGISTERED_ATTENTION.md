## Code/document and attribution distinctions

- `xpq.exp.villages_5`, `xpq.exp.structures_15`, and `xpq.exp.grand_expedition` are the material **catalog semantics versus implementation** mismatch: A's `R` retroactivity differs from safe forward-only implementation. It follows the later reliable-evidence owner policy; owner must approve any alternative migration.
- For registered combat rows with `stat_killed*`, vanilla `ENTITY_KILLED` records the final blow's causing entity; install-forward `counter` kill-credit rows use the death hook and `getKillCredit()`. Tamed-wolf or environmental follow-up kills can therefore differ by objective. The distinction is encoded and tested, but needs clear copy and live multiplayer checks before approval.
- `xpq.boss.monument_clear` and `xpq.boss.monuments_3` are **not** in the 75. They have automated monument tests and remain VERIFY/unregistered, so their implementation evidence does not promote their content status.
- No gameplay prerequisite was found in the registered quest rules; PR #129 presentation links must not be read as unlock gates.
