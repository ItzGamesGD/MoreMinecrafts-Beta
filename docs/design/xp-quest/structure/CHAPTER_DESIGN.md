# XP Quest 200-Quest Structure Proposal

Status: **PROPOSAL, owner review required.** This is presentation data only. It registers no quests, changes no rewards, enables no prerequisites, and is not loaded by the runtime. The production tree still shows the three Ecology quests.

Generated by `scripts/xp_quest/generate_structure_proposal.py` from `src/main/resources/data/moreminecrafts/xp_quest/catalog.json`. Re-run the script after any catalog change; `QuestStructureProposalTest` fails if the proposal drifts from the catalog.

## 1. Verified facts (current HEAD)

- 200 catalog quests, approved raw XP total 209,100 (180,000 active, 29,100 future).
- Category counts match the brief: exploration 24, dimension 10, combat 30, boss 9, mining 19, farming 19, fishing 16, crafting 15, building 8, alchemy 9, equipment 20, growth 21.
- **Status and registration differ from the brief's expectations.** The catalog has NOW 163, VERIFY 17, FUTURE 20. Only 75 quests are `registered: true`. The brief's earlier figure of 160 NOW registered individually is not what the catalog records. The proposal keeps the catalog's values unchanged.
- **Localization gap (blocking for the production tree):** only 3 of 200 quests have copy (title, description, objective) in either language. The other 197 have none. This proposal does not invent quest copy. Chapter and sub-branch titles are supplied in both languages (see §3).

## 2. Concepts kept separate

1. **Presentation grouping:** quests shown together in a chapter or sub-branch. Carries no gameplay meaning.
2. **Suggested progression:** visual reading order (for example, a kill-count ladder). It never restricts play.
3. **Actual prerequisites:** gameplay gates. None is enabled. Each candidate needs explicit owner approval and rule verification.

Design choices:
- **Ecology is preserved** as chapter 1 with its three quests in their current order.
- **Combat is split** into Monster Hunt, Arms and Tactics, and Elite Foes, so 30 combat quests do not form one long lane.
- **Bosses are a capstone** and sit last, because most boss quests are MID_LATE or later.
- **Planned and verify quests stay in place** and are marked by sub-branch name, not hidden. Their status is shown in the mapping and must render as locked or planned (see §6).
- **Icons are vanilla items** chosen to match each chapter's theme. Each is an existing `minecraft:` item id and must be validated against the registry before use.

## 4. Mapping and ordering

`quest_structure_mapping.json` contains one entry per quest: `quest_id`, `category`, `chapter`, `sub_branch`, `presentation_order` (1–200, contiguous, chapter-contiguous), `chapter_index`, `status`, `tier`, `registered`, `reward_raw_xp`. Every fact except the chapter and sub-branch assignment is copied from the catalog, and the test checks each copy.

## 5. Dependency ledger (`edge_ledger.json`)

| Kind | Count | Enforcement | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `suggested_progression` | 21 | presentation-only | proposed |
| `cross_chapter_presentation` | 3 | presentation-only | proposed |
| `gameplay_candidate` | 2 | gameplay-enforced, **NOT ENABLED** | awaiting owner approval |

Presentation-only edges never block or unlock a claim, so they have no retroactive effect. The two gameplay candidates (T2 and T3 equipment gated by Research tiers) are **not verified** against current authority. The docs mention knowledge gates in general, but I did not find a rule confirming these specific gates. If enabled, the rules are: a child already claimed keeps its claim; an unclaimed child waits for its parent; a parent completed before activation counts immediately; no XP is granted retroactively.

Edges are not chains for their own sake. Most suggested edges are count ladders within one tracker family. The cross-chapter links are reading-order only.

## 6. Runtime gate (not applied)

Before any integration with the playable tree:
- **Registered and playable** (`registered: true`, status NOW): claimable as today.
- **Unregistered NOW, VERIFY and FUTURE quests** must never be completable, claimable or reward-bearing because they appear in presentation data. Integration must filter on registration, and the claim path must reject unregistered ids.
- **Showing unavailable quests** needs a separate approval. Planned locked states must be specified before they render.

The current `QuestTreeStructure` ignores status. The production tree is still built from three ids, so this gate is a precondition, not something already in place.

## 10. Known limitations

- The proposal is not validated against a rendered client. Pan, zoom, tooltip containment and navigation are unverified for 200 nodes.
- Sub-branches are not yet rendered as separate groups in the runtime canvas. The mapping and previews show them; the layout still treats each chapter as one lane.
- Quest copy is missing for 197 quests in both languages.
- The gameplay candidates are unverified against authority.
- Icons are chosen but not validated against the registry.
- Counts in the brief for NOW and VERIFY differ from the catalog (see §1).
