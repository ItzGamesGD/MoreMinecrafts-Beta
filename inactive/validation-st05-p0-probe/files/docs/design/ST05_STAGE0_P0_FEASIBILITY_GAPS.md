### Validity and invalidation procedure (proposal only)

1. Verify authority/artifact/lineage, complete natural membership, identity, qualification, ≤8 pre-roll fit evidence, original roll and full greedy completeness/rank before an A03 attempt. Distinguish genuine pre-roll no-fit from later invalidated evidence.
2. Capture complete live read/write and indirect dependencies and validate their versions against independent provenance. Include other accepted plans as writers; pairwise write-mask nonoverlap does not imply joint validity.
3. On any relevant accepted-plan/player/fluid/structure/offline change, revoke every affected certificate/fence before further effects; recursively expand affected proof/quarantine dependencies. Unknown closure expands uncertainty, never certifies independence. Avoid mutual lock-wait cycles; no liveness bound established.
4. **Before first write:** keep original winner, accepted-decision provenance, original selected plan and rank; mark invalid-proof pending. Revalidate the exact original semantics only if independently sound. Changed fit/plan/rank/population needs explicit review, not hidden post-roll reroll, replacement, fresh candidate search or post-roll fit lottery.
5. **After writes begin:** stop stale effects, preserve actual/player data and quarantine conservative dependencies. Recover only independent proven-safe portions; otherwise logically retire incomplete attempt and preserve protection. A partial effective group remains strict integrity FAIL even with perfect quarantine/retirement.
6. **After genuine publication:** generation is historical fact. Normal player architecture/NBT edits persist; generation machinery must not repair, retire, decrement success or allocate a new roll merely because current state differs. Trial validation/lifecycle follows existing authority separately.
7. On restart, reconcile retained original decisions, publication evidence, quarantine/protection/fences and accounting **before** new placement/controller effects. Rebuild missing projections idempotently; do not infer success/nonintervention from block equality or infer empty index ranges from absence.

DEP-01 gives a concrete logical counterexample: P removes support r which Q reads outside Q's write mask; both passed against the old snapshot and their direct write maps do not overlap. After P's change, Q's old proof is invalid and Q must remain pending with zero stale placement/publication. DEP-02 extends this to a seal→fluid→dry-floor dependency. Actual Minecraft reachability consistent with chosen spacing/footprints is UNKNOWN until those parameters/geometry are frozen; record a proof if a proposed example is impossible rather than presenting it as a reproduced world failure.
